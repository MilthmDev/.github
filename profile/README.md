## 关于 / About

Milthm 是一款**免费、非商业**的有轨下落式音乐节奏游戏。雨落在梦的边界，音符随雨声落下——我们希望用音乐、谱面与故事，把每一场雨变成一次可以反复回味的旅程。

*Milthm is a **free, non-commercial** vertical-scrolling rhythm game. Rain falls at the edge of a dream, and notes fall with the sound of rain. We use music, charts, and stories to turn every rainfall into a journey worth revisiting.*

### 游戏特色 / Features

- **雨之声符 / Rain Notes** — 雨露、雨丝、雨滴、闪电等多类音符，带来独特的读谱与操作体验。<br>
  *Drizzle, slide, drop, and lightning notes for a distinctive reading and play experience.*

- **四档难度 / Four Difficulties** — Drizzle、Sprinkle、Cloudburst、Clear，另有 Special 谱面。<br>
  *Drizzle, Sprinkle, Cloudburst, and Clear, plus Special charts.*

- **丰富内容 / Rich Content** — 主线与支线章节、单曲、联动曲包、剧情与角色。<br>
  *Main and side chapters, singles, collaboration packs, stories, and characters.*

- **跨平台 / Cross-Platform** — iOS / iPadOS、Android、Windows、macOS、Linux。<br>
  *iOS / iPadOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux.*

- **在线系统 / Online Features** — 云存档、成绩排行、回放与花园系统。<br>
  *Cloud saves, leaderboards, replays, and the garden system.*
